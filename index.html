ℹ️ About MindVault

A place designed for honest thoughts.

What is MindVault?

MindVault is an anonymous emotional-support community concept where people can write what they are feeling without using their real name.

How does it work?

Posts and replies are stored online in Firebase so the community can see the same conversation.

Important

This is a school project and is not professional medical or psychological advice.

❤️ Need help?

It's okay to ask for support.

Talk to someone

Consider speaking with a trusted friend, family member, teacher, counselor, or mental-health professional.

In an emergency

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

Be kind

When replying, be supportive and respectful.
